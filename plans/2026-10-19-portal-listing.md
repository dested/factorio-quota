## Description (paste as Markdown)

**An hour-long Factorio roguelite.** The Company drops you on a tiny island with a Shipping Terminal, a starter kit and a quota. Every shift the clock runs and the quota climbs. Make the number and you get a break, a paycheck and a shop. Miss it and you get a written warning. Miss it again and you're fired.

It's still Factorio (ore, belts, assemblers, power, the tech tree), just with a boss watching the clock.

**Play it from New Game → Scenarios → QUOTA.** The mod does nothing in any other game, so it's safe to leave enabled.

### A run

- **Shifts:** a timer and a quota. Anything in the Shipping Terminal sells at market price, live. Crafted goods pay more than their ingredients.
- **The market moves:** a few items run hot each shift (x2 to x3), and selling too much of one thing floods its price. The MARKET panel does the maths for you.
- **Breaks:** shop the Company Catalog.
  - **Contracts:** 30 rule-benders, five slots, three offers per break.
  - **Tech:** Factorio's own research, bought outright.
  - **Supplies:** machines by orbital drop pod.
  - **Land:** new plots rise out of the sea.
- **Conditions:** gremlins, earthquakes, union strikes, meteor showers, a solar eclipse, biter raids, picky clients, and more.
- **The Audit:** the final shift has a boss. Survive it to be promoted, then keep going in Endless.

### Simple or Complex

| | Simple | Complex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 8 shifts, about an hour | 10 shifts, about 80 minutes |
| Prices | Follow ingredient cost | Deep chains pay up to 2.6x |
| Tech | Everything for sale | Zoned by land you own |

**First time? Play Simple.**

Everything else is on the setup screen:
- Difficulty.
- Every threat, condition and event, one switch each.
- Presets, including **Race the Clock**: no threats at all, just you, the market and the timer.
- Run length, warnings, quota size, shift length and break length.

### Compatibility

- Factorio 2.1.
- Works with or without Space Age, Quality, Elevated Rails and Recycler. The run happens on its own island, and other planets' resources are left out of the economy.
- Single player.

### Made with an AI

The code and the balance were written by Claude, working with one human who kept playing it and saying what felt wrong. Every fix in the changelog came out of a real run. Bug reports and "this felt bad" notes are very welcome in the discussion tab.
