Status: active (2026-10-19)

From Sal's first long playtest. Packaging (scenario, Space Age compat, README, mod portal) follows
as a second pass once v2 plays well.

## Fixes

- **$1 ammo.** Company property was a fungible counter (250 magazines from the kits), so freshly
  crafted ammo sold for scrap after the kit ammo was fired. Counter removed. Company handouts that
  are meant to be used (ammo, coal, repair packs, modules, grenades) arrive as `quota-issued-*`
  copies: same function, stamped icon, no value, terminal won't take them. Care packages carry
  machines only. Crafted goods always sell at full price.
- **Shift-2 meteor storms.** Meteor Shower and Gremlin Infestation move to the tier-2 pool
  (shift 4+). Showers thin out early (gap grows), early meteors are a lighter projectile. Starter
  kit adds 20 repair packs; `create_ghost_on_entity_death` from the start.
- **Kit:** 2 boilers, 2 steam engines, 100 coal. Power crate becomes a small Steam Expansion.
- **HUD:** compact panel, bottom-right by default; per-player setting for position.
- **Terminal art** sat a tile high on its 6x6 box.
- **Recipe on hover:** item icons carry `elem_tooltip` = the recipe that priced the item.

## Run Setup (replaces the difficulty pick)

Mode (Simple / Complex) + difficulty (Intern / Associate / Crunch) + threats (preset + per-item
toggles) + fine print (run length, quota x, shift length x, break length, warnings). Backed by
runtime-global mod settings, so the main menu sets defaults and a mid-run change applies from
the next shift (mode, difficulty and run length lock at TAKE THE JOB).

Threat presets: Full Company (all on), No Biters (raids, nests, Audit off), Race the Clock
(every threat off; market conditions and good events stay).

## Simple vs Complex

| | Simple | Complex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shifts | 8 | 10 |
| Economy | flat markup | Complexity Pays: value x (0.6 + 0.2 x depth), depth = recipe wave |
| Tech | all R&D open | Zoning: oil tier needs 2 bought plots, blue tier 4, purple/yellow 6 |
| Quotas | table | table, +5%/shift from shift 4, x1.4/shift beyond 8 |

Zoning tiers come from each tech's science packs (red/green 1, black 2, blue 3, purple/yellow 4)
plus the oil branch (oil-processing and descendants) at least 2. Locked techs are disabled in the
vanilla tree too, so labs can't bypass it.

## Handbook

Window (from Run Setup and as a Catalog tab) listing contracts, conditions, events, R&D +
upgrades, supplies, land and corporate offers. Conditions and events show their switch.
