Version: 2.0.0
Date: 2026-10-19
  Features:
    - QUOTA is now a scenario: New Game > Scenarios > QUOTA. The mod does nothing in any other game, so it can stay in your mod list.
    - Run Setup screen: Simple mode (8 shifts, all tech for sale) or Complex mode (10 shifts, deep production chains pay up to 2.6x, tech zoned by how much land you own).
    - Every threat, condition and event can be switched off. Presets: Full Company, No Biters, Race the Clock.
    - Fine print: run length, written warnings, quota size, shift length and break length. All of it lives in Map settings too.
    - The Company Handbook: every contract, condition, event, tech, crate, plot type and corporate offer in one window.
    - MARKET side panel: hot items worked out for you (usual price x multiplier = what it pays now) and the top payers, live.
    - Hover an item icon for its recipe.
    - Clock out early once the quota is met, for a bonus on the time left.
    - Electric Furnaces in the Tech tab.
    - Works with Space Age, Quality, Elevated Rails and Recycler enabled.
  Changes:
    - Compact HUD in the bottom-right corner (per-player setting for the corner).
    - Starter kit: 2 boilers, 2 steam engines, 100 coal and 20 repair packs. More power is for sale.
    - Meteor showers and gremlin infestations wait until shift 4; early meteors are smaller and rarer.
    - Destroyed machines leave ghosts.
    - Shotgun shells pay less and are never hot.
    - Drop pods raise an alert when they land.
    - A Shipping Terminal holds its sale while you have it open.
    - The R&D tab is now Tech, with a note on how it relates to Factorio's own tech tree.
    - The Land tab only shows your land and the plots for sale next to it.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed crafted ammo selling for $1 while shown as hot.
    - Fixed the Shipping Terminal art sitting a tile above its footprint.
